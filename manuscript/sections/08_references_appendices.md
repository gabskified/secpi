## ⚠️ Appendix B does not belong in a journal submission

Process documentation — advisor consultation photos, project-management screenshots, literature-search tooling — is standard for a school capstone and reads as unprofessional in a Q1 geoscience venue. It also creates two specific problems:

1. The **QGIS rasterization of Caloocan People's Park** implies a real georeferenced study site. The manuscript's central scope claim is that it is synthetic and non-georeferenced (Flags #3, #10, #11). A reviewer who sees this will question the scope statement.
2. The **70 × 70 m initial heatmaps** are from the superseded `INITIALCODE` pipeline and do not correspond to the 100 × 100 m two-level grid described in Methods.

Recommend removing Appendix B entirely, or retaining only genuinely scientific content. Consultation records can be acknowledged in the Acknowledgements.

Term Definition
Cumulative Crown Area The total area covered by the combined canopies of all trees in a given configuration. This
acts as a biophysical constraint in the model, identifying and limiting "canopy overlap" and
competitive crowding between trees.
Gaussian Decay Model A mathematical function where cooling intensity is strongest at the tree center and
diminishes following a bell curve toward the edges.
Moore Neighborhood A neighborhood defined on a lattice and composed of a cell and its eight surrounding cells
as adjacent (neighbor) to it.
Multi-storied Canopy A complex forest structure common in tropical environments (like the Philippines)
composed of emergent trees, a dominant canopy, understory layers, and forest-floor
vegetation. This creates high vertical stratification and spatial variability, requiring larger
study plots (at least 100 x 100 m²) for accurate biomass and cooling estimation.
Performance Cliff
(Species-Specific)
A sharp decline in cooling effectiveness (measured by the SECPI score) when
high-performing species are missing.
Pheromone Concentration A digital trail left by "ants" that represents the quality of a specific tree configuration;
higher concentrations guide future iterations toward better solutions.
Shading Dominance The finding that a tree’s physical shade area is a significantly more powerful cooling factor
than its biological evapotranspiration.
Stigmergic Learning The mechanism by which the algorithm uses environmental markers (pheromones) to solve
the non-convex optimization problem.

Appendix C: Open-Source Computational Framework and Reproducibility
To facilitate reproducibility and support the further development of the Equitable Integer Lattice Optimization Paradigm, the
complete Python implementation of this study has been archived in a public repository. The codebase includes the following core
components:
1. Cellular Automata Generator: The module responsible for procedural generation of the coarse 100x100m urban grid
(TwoLevelUrbanGrid class) and morphological synthesis.
2. Biophysical Cooling Proxy: The implementation of the Gaussian decay cooling model, including the specific allometric
parameters for the six Philippine Tree Functional Types.
3. Optimization Engine: The VariableRandomnessACO class containing the Ant Colony Optimization logic,
pheromone update rules, and sensitivity analysis scripts.
4. Data and Visualization: The JSON configuration files used for the experimental runs and the scripts used to generate
the heatmaps and convergence plots presented in the results.
Researchers and urban planners may access the repository, view the documentation, and download the source code via the link or
QR code provided below.
